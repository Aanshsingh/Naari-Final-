Our Story

Our Heritage

Naari was born from a deep-seated love for the rhythmic click of the loom and the silent poetry of a needle passing through silk. We believe that every garment carries the heartbeat of its creator.

Our journey began in the small clusters of Banaras and Lucknow, where generations of master artisans have preserved secrets of craftsmanship that no machine could ever replicate. We don't just make clothes; we preserve a legacy of hand-woven excellence.

Social Impact

"Empowering the hands that weave our dreams."

At Naari, we operate on a model of radical transparency. By eliminating middlemen, we ensure that our weavers receive a fair living wage, healthcare for their families, and the dignity they deserve. 15% of every purchase is directly reinvested into the 'Vankar' foundation to support the education of the next generation of artisans.
